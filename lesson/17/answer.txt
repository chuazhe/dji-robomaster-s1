How do you create an empty list in Python?
Answer: my_list = []

How do you add an element to the end of a list?
Answer: my_list.append(5)

How do you access the first element of a list?
Answer: my_list[0]

How do you remove an element from a list by value?
Answer: my_list.remove(5)

How do you find the length of a list?
Answer: len(my_list)

How do you combine two lists into one?
Answer: combined = list1 + list2

How do you check if an item exists in a list?
Answer: 5 in my_list

How do you get a sublist containing the first three elements?
Answer: my_list[:3]

How do you reverse the elements of a list?
Answer: my_list.reverse() or my_list[::-1]

How do you sort a list in ascending order?
Answer: my_list.sort() or sorted(my_list)
