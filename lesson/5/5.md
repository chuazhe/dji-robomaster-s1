* Must start with a letter or an underscore (_). They cannot start with a number (e.g., 2age is invalid).
* Can only contain alpha-numeric characters and underscores. Special characters like spaces, dashes (-), $, or % are not allowed.
* Cannot be Python keywords (reserved words like if, else, for, class, print, etc.).
* Are case-sensitive. age, Age, and AGE are considered three different, distinct variables.
